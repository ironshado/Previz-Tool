# Previz-Tool
A browser-based 3D shot planning tool so directors can block scenes, place characters, and frame AI video prompts before a single pixel gets generated. Bring your imagination to life


USAGE GUIDE


1. Pick a scene — Choose Cafe, Park, Office, Studio or Street from the top-left dropdown. The environment loads instantly.
2. Place characters — Hit Spawn Man or Spawn Woman. Click a character in the left panel to select it, then use Move / Rotate / Scale to position them.
3. Frame your shot — Navigate the left panel with Middle Mouse to orbit, Right Mouse to pan, and Scroll to zoom. The right panel previews exactly what your shot camera sees in real time.
4. Lock your frame — Click ⛓ FREE in the preview header to decouple the cameras, freezing your shot while you continue exploring the scene.
5. Annotate — Draw arrows, sketch notes, or drop text labels directly on the preview using the doodle tools in the bottom-right.
6. Export — Hit Capture Shot to download a JPG with your framing, annotations, scene name, and camera data baked in.
Keyboard shortcuts: G = Move · R = Rotate · S = Scale · F = Frame selection · Esc = Deselect
